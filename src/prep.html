SOX Tester Foundations: Prep Course, Days 6 to 11

TIB Systems LLC · SOX Tester Crash Course · Taught in class on Days 6 to 11, each idea from the beginning

This course is taught in class, one lesson a day, on Days 6 to 11 of the main course. Your instructor teaches each lesson with you. You do not need SAP or audit experience. You need Excel, the three hours of each class day and the willingness to explain things out loud. All people, companies and numbers in it are made up for teaching.

How to work each lesson

  1. Recall (15 min): write yesterday’s topic from memory, then check.
  2. Read (45 min): take notes in your own words; write three questions first.
  3. Do (75 min): the exercises, before you look at the answers.
  4. Teach back (20 min): record the teach-back at the end of the lesson.
  5. Pass it on (25 min): teach one idea to a colleague.

The six lessons

Day 6

Lesson 1: Meet the method

Learn: The five-step loop, the 7-part teach-back, the rubric, your tracker, mistakes log and recordings folder.

Do: Exercises 1.1 to 1.3 and a practice teach-back.

Done when: Tracker set up; one teach-back recorded and scored.

Day 7

Lesson 2: Data, evidence and the test list

Learn: Population and sample, complete versus accurate, Excel for testers, the SAP tables, the four-part extract standard, the 42 tests.

Do: Exercises 2.1 to 2.5. Excel: dormant users, leavers, emergency access, reconciliation, messy data.

Done when: Gate 1: you can prove a list complete and write a data request.

Day 8

Lesson 3: The tester role, the four ITGC domains and phases 0 to 4

Learn: SOX 404, control types, the four domains, planning phases, test attributes, sample sizes and a repeatable random sample.

Do: Exercises 3.1 to 3.5. Excel: the random sample and the sample-size calculator.

Done when: You can write attributes and choose a sample size from the rule.

Day 9

Lesson 4: Phases 5 to 11 and classifying deficiencies

Learn: Testing, SOC 1 reports, bridge periods, exceptions in seven steps, deficiency levels, the five-part finding, roll-forward and close-out.

Do: Exercises 4.1 to 4.4 and a recording.

Done when: You can turn an exception into a classified finding.

Day 10

Lesson 5: The evidence standard and the full roadmap

Learn: The four-part evidence standard, strong versus weak evidence, the 12 phases with one output each, role fit.

Do: Exercises 5.1 to 5.4 (mock Gate 2). Excel: transports and a second pass on users and leavers.

Done when: Gate 2: four domains, 12 phases and the evidence standard without notes.

Day 11

Lesson 6: Bridge to the labs, your first complete test

Learn: How every lab is laid out, the working paper, tickmarks, the warm-up test, a full supplier-approval test and finding, the six gates.

Do: Exercise 6.1 and Recording 6. Excel: manual journals.

Done when: Ready for Day 12 of the main course.
