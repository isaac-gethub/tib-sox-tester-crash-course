What You Will Do: Labs and Exercises in Order

TIB Systems LLC · SOX Tester Crash Course · Trainee guide

Only what you do, in course order. Tap a test ID to open its full procedure in the reader app (opens in a new tab; sign in with your course account). Your instructor tells you which items to open each day.

1. Days 1 to 5: the Prep Block, taught in class

These are the first five class days of the course. Your instructor teaches each day with you. The plan (item 9.1) is read on Day 1, and the gate task closes Day 5. The guides for these days are titled Day P1 to Day P5; P1 is Day 1, P2 is Day 2, and so on.

Day 1

Block plan and the two routes

Read the plan and choose how you will get your data: Route 1 (extract it yourself in SAP) or Route 2 (client-supplied data).

You produce: Your chosen route

Where to look: Prep Block Plan, item 9.1

Day 1

Excel handling

Load a file safely, fix real dates, trim text, remove duplicates and prove the record count on the practice file (Employee Extract).

You produce: Clean file with a proven count

Where to look: Day P1 guide, item 9.2 practice file, item 9.3

Day 2

Excel testing formulas

Work the 11-task drill: counts, lookups, business days and a PivotTable on access, leaver and change data.

You produce: Completed drill workbook

Where to look: Day P2 guide, item 9.4 practice workbook, item 9.5

Day 3

Route 1: extract it yourself

Practise the evidence habit: system, client, date and time, parameters, record count. Complete an extraction log.

You produce: Extraction log

Where to look: Day P3 guide, item 9.6

Day 4

Route 2: client-supplied data

Write the request for the data, then validate a client-supplied file for completeness and accuracy.

You produce: Request and validated file

Where to look: Day P4 guide, item 9.7 practice workbook, item 9.8

Day 5

Evidence output

Use the workpaper template: record the result, log exceptions and write a five-part finding.

You produce: Workpaper and finding

Where to look: Day P5 guide, item 9.9 workpaper template, item 9.10

Day 5

Firefighter review

Prep Block gate task: review the firefighter data against the criteria and write up the result.

You produce: Gate workpaper, scored by your instructor

Where to look: gate task data, item 9.11

Foundations lessons: taught with Days 6 to 11

The Foundations course teaches each idea from the beginning. Its six lessons are taught in class on Days 6 to 11, one lesson a day, in place of the usual reading and exercises for those days. They are numbered separately, so they are called Lesson 1 to 6 here. The student guide, practice workbook and Excel files are items 1.14 to 1.17 in the Days 6 to 25 room.

Lesson 1

Meet the method

The five-step loop, the seven-part teach-back, the rubric, your tracker, mistakes log and recordings folder. Do exercises 1.1 to 1.3 and a practice teach-back.

You produce: Tracker set up; one teach-back recorded and scored

Lesson 2

Data, evidence and the test list

Population and sample, complete versus accurate, Excel for testers, the four-part extract standard and the 42 tests. Do exercises 2.1 to 2.5 on dormant users, leavers, emergency access, reconciliation and messy data.

You produce: Gate 1: prove a list complete and write a data request

Lesson 3

The tester role, the four ITGC domains and phases 0 to 4

SOX 404, control types, planning phases, test attributes, sample sizes and a repeatable random sample. Do exercises 3.1 to 3.5.

You produce: Test attributes and a sample size chosen from the rule

Lesson 4

Phases 5 to 11 and classifying deficiencies

Testing, SOC 1 reports, bridge periods, exceptions in seven steps, deficiency levels and the five-part finding. Do exercises 4.1 to 4.4 and a recording.

You produce: An exception turned into a classified finding

Lesson 5

The evidence standard and the full roadmap

The four-part evidence standard, strong versus weak evidence and the 12 phases with one output each. Do exercises 5.1 to 5.4 (mock Gate 2).

You produce: Gate 2 without notes: four domains, 12 phases, evidence standard

Lesson 6

Bridge to the labs: your first complete test

How every lab is laid out, the working paper, tickmarks, the warm-up test and a full supplier-approval test with its finding. Do exercise 6.1 and recording 6.

You produce: Ready for Day 12 of the main course
